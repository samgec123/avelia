Help & FAQs

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also check your order status in My Account.

What is your return policy?

We offer free returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. International delivery times and costs are calculated at checkout.

How do I find my size?

Visit our Size Guide for detailed measurements and fit advice for each category.

Can I amend or cancel my order?

Orders can be amended or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. Please contact us immediately at orders@aurelia.com.
