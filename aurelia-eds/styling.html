How to Wear It

The Art of Dressing

Ideas on how to build and wear a considered wardrobe.

The considered wardrobe is built, not bought. It starts with a few pieces you reach for constantly — a shirt, a pair of trousers, a coat — and grows from there, slowly, with intention.

Our styling notes are not trend reports. They're observations about how clothes work together and how to get the most from what you own. The goal is a wardrobe that works for every occasion without having to think too hard.

The uniform is not repetition. It is freedom.
