Our Materials

We source only what endures — fibres that age with grace and improve with wear.

Every material in our collection is chosen for its longevity and its relationship with the body. We avoid synthetic blends where natural fibres will perform better, and we work only with mills who share our standards.

Wool & Cashmere — Sourced from certified sustainable farms in New Zealand and Scotland. Our knitwear is made in Yorkshire.

Linen — Belgian and Irish linen, grown and processed without chemicals. Gets softer with every wash.

Silk — OEKO-TEX certified silk from Italy. Woven to our own specifications for weight and drape.

Cotton — GOTS certified organic cotton. Woven in Portugal and Japan.
