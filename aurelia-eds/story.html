The Story

How a belief in considered dressing became a wardrobe for life.

It began in 2012 with a single coat. Not a coat designed to be noticed, but one designed to last — to be worn on every journey, in every season, for every year. That coat still exists in our archive.

Our founders believed that the fashion industry had lost its way, chasing novelty at the expense of craft. They set out to build something different: a house of essentials, each piece made with the intention of becoming the last one you'll ever need of its kind.

Twelve years on, that philosophy guides everything we do. We release four small collections a year, each built around a single material or silhouette, considered from sketch to delivery.
