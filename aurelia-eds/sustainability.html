Our Commitment

Made Responsibly

Sustainability isn't a campaign. It's how we make things.

We don't make sustainability claims lightly. What we do is simpler: we make fewer things, better, from materials that last. We work only with manufacturers who meet our standards for fair wages and working conditions.

All our natural fibre pieces are made from certified organic or responsibly-sourced materials. Our packaging is entirely recycled and recyclable. We've eliminated single-use plastics from our supply chain.

We're not perfect. But we report honestly on our progress every year — including what we haven't yet achieved.
