About Us

A study in modern essentials. Designed in Milan, made to last beyond the season.

AURELIA was founded on a single belief: that the most considered wardrobe is one built slowly, with intention. Each piece we create is designed in Milan and produced in small quantities by artisans who share our commitment to craft.

We design for the long term — clothes that move with you, age with you, and resist the pressure of trends. Our palette draws from nature: taupes, creams, rusts, navies and deep olives that work together and wear together.

Every material is selected for its longevity. We work with heritage mills in Italy, Portugal, and Japan, choosing fabrics that improve with wear rather than diminish.
